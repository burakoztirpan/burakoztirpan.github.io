We collect and process data strictly to provide and optimize the inspection workflow:

  • Personal Info & Account Data: Your email address and unique User IDs are processed securely via Firebase Authentication and RevenueCat for account management, authentication, and subscription tracking.
  • Structured Inspection Data (Cloud Storage): Your project data, room lists, checklists, structural findings, and user notes are stored securely using Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Visual Data (Local-Only Storage): Your privacy regarding visual assets is strictly protected. All inspection photos and images remain entirely on your local device storage. We do not host, store, or back up your raw image files on our servers.
