What is Sahih Bukhari?

Hadith Vault is a free iPhone app for reading the complete Sahih al-Bukhari hadith collection, with 97 books and more than 7,200 hadiths, in English, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian and Turkish. It works fully offline, needs no account, and includes a hadith of the day, a daily reading streak, favorites, reminders and shareable hadith cards.

Download Sahih Bukhari: Hadith Vault on the App Store

Is there a Sahih Bukhari app in Urdu or Indonesian?

Yes. Hadith Vault includes the full Sahih al-Bukhari in Urdu, Indonesian, English, Arabic and Turkish, and every screen of the app is translated, not only the hadith text.

Can I read Sahih al-Bukhari offline?

Yes. The whole collection is stored in the app, so Hadith Vault works without an internet connection.

How many hadiths does Hadith Vault include?

It contains the complete Sahih al-Bukhari: 97 books and more than 7,200 hadiths, with searchable book titles and hadith numbers.

Does Hadith Vault have a daily hadith?

Yes. A new hadith is featured every day, you can build a daily reading streak, and an optional local reminder notifies you at a time you choose.

Is Hadith Vault free?

Yes, Hadith Vault is free to download and use. It shows ads, and an optional in-app purchase removes them. No account is needed.
