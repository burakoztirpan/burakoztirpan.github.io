Hello, this is Burak.

I build independent iOS apps from Istanbul. All of them are free to download on the App Store and keep your data on your device whenever possible.

  • Tensi: Stress & HRV Tracker

    Tensi is a stress and HRV (heart rate variability) tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. It connects your calendar, the places you visit, the weather and your sleep with HRV and resting heart rate from Apple Health, so you can see which meetings, days and habits drain you and which help you recover. All analysis runs on your device, with no account and no server.

    App Store

  • Sahih Bukhari: Hadith Vault

    Hadith Vault is a free iPhone app for reading the complete Sahih al-Bukhari hadith collection, with 97 books and more than 7,200 hadiths, in English, Arabic, Urdu, Indonesian and Turkish. It works fully offline, needs no account, and includes a hadith of the day, a daily reading streak, favorites, reminders and shareable hadith cards.

    App Store

  • Habiproof: Home Inspection

    Habiproof is a home and property inspection app for iPhone and Android. It turns photos, room-by-room checklists and notes into a clean, branded PDF inspection report on site. AI suggests checklist items, defects and severity, and you review every suggestion before it is saved. It is built for home inspectors, landlords, letting agents and property managers.

    App Store

  • Undelay: Beat Procrastination

    Undelay is a to-do list and habit app for iPhone and iPad that helps you beat procrastination. Every task is a promise to yourself. Undelay tracks what you finish, postpone and drop, and turns it into one honest number, your Kept Rate, so you can see where you follow through and where you keep delaying. It works without an account and stores everything on your device.

    App Store

  • NeonBurn: Calorie Burn Workout

    NeonBurn is a camera-based cardio workout app for iPhone and iPad. Prop up your phone, step back and move: the camera counts every rep of 17 standing moves, including jumping jacks, squats, high knees, punches and front kicks, shows you as a neon skeleton and adds up the calories you burn. No equipment, no wearable and no account are needed.

    App Store

  • Voxbug Run: Kids Motion Game

    Voxbug Run is an active motion game for kids aged 4 to 10 on iPhone and iPad. Children stand in front of the device and jump, duck, step left or right and run on the spot, and a little voxel bug copies their moves as it races through six garden worlds. The camera only watches for moves; nothing is recorded. There are no ads and no tracking.

    App Store

  • Natusay: Kids Nature Explorer

    Natusay is an AI nature explorer for kids on iPhone. Your child points the camera at a bug, bird, frog, plant or tree, and Natusay identifies it and turns it into a friendly character with a name, voice and portrait that answers questions in short, simple sentences. It is designed to be used with a parent and includes a parent gate, daily time limits and readable chat history.

    App Store
