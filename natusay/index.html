Snap.

Point the camera at a bug, a bird, a frog, even a tree. Radar mode beeps faster and buzzes as something interesting gets close, so finding a friend feels like a little hunt, and sneaking up slowly pays off.

No camera handy? Pick a photo from the library.

Meet.

Natusay works out what is in the picture and invents a character to match: a name, a personality, a voice, a freshly drawn portrait. It says hello and tells you what it is up to right now.

Recognition can be wrong. Parents can fix a friend's name or species any time.

Talk.

Hold the button and ask anything: what do you eat, where do you live, can you fly? Answers come back in short, simple sentences, and the character stays in its own little world. Every friend remembers the last chat.

Not sure what to ask? Tap a suggestion.

Fun for kids. Calm for parents.

Is it poisonous? Can I touch it? Can I eat it? The answer is always: ask a grown-up.

Parent gate
Purchases, settings and deleting friends sit behind a quick multiplication question.
Daily time limit
Choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. A calm rest screen appears when time is up, and you can grant 15 more.
Every chat, readable
Open the Parent Area to read what was said and correct a friend's name, species or caution level.
Private by design
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Photos, friends and chats stay on the iPhone.
Reminders only if you want them
A daily nudge is available in the Parent Area and off by default.

To recognize a photo and write a reply, the photo and the chat text are processed by AI services. The privacy policy spells out exactly what goes where.

A reason to go outside.

  • Daily quests and badges

    “Find 3 flowers!” A fresh quest every day, with a progress tracker. Finishing one means meeting friends of the right kind, so the phone is the excuse and the garden is the point. Badges and a quest history keep the wins.

  • Nature hints

    Photograph a patch of grass, a tree trunk or a pond edge and get three gentle hints on what might live there and where to look. Observation only, never touching.

  • Listen mode

    Hold the phone up and Natusay listens for birds, buzzing insects and croaking frogs. The sound is analyzed on the iPhone and never recorded.

  • My Friends

    Everyone you meet is kept with their photo, portrait and chat. Filter by bugs, birds or plants and heart your favorites.

  • Did you know?

    Each friend carries a few true, kid-sized facts, and the home screen surfaces one a day.

Questions? Ask us

What is Natusay?

Natusay is an AI nature explorer for kids on iPhone. Your child points the camera at a bug, bird, frog, plant or tree, and Natusay identifies it and turns it into a friendly character with a name, voice and portrait that answers questions in short, simple sentences. It is designed to be used with a parent and includes a parent gate, daily time limits and readable chat history.

Download Natusay: Kids Nature Explorer on the App Store

Is there a safe AI app for kids to learn about nature?

Natusay is built for this. Characters stay in their own little world, answer in short sentences, and always tell children to ask a grown-up about safety questions like touching or eating something. Parents can read every chat and set daily time limits.

Can Natusay identify insects and animals from a photo?

Yes. Natusay recognises bugs, birds, frogs, plants and other things kids find outdoors from a photo. Recognition can be wrong, so parents can correct a friend's name or species at any time.

What ages is Natusay for?

Natusay is made for young children exploring outdoors together with a parent.

Is Natusay free?

Yes. Three scans and twenty chats a day are free. A Natusay Premium subscription removes the limits.
