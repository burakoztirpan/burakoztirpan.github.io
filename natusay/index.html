Snap.

Point the camera at a bug, a bird, a tree, even a rock. Radar mode beeps faster and buzzes as something interesting gets close, so finding a friend feels like a little hunt, and sneaking up slowly pays off.

No camera handy? Pick a photo from the library.

Meet.

Natusay works out what is in the picture and invents a character to match: a name, a personality, a voice, a freshly drawn portrait. It says hello and tells you what it is up to right now.

Recognition can be wrong. Parents can fix a friend's name or species any time.

Talk.

Hold the button and ask anything: what do you eat, where do you live, can you fly? Answers come back in short, simple sentences, and the character stays in its own little world. Every friend remembers the last chat.

Not sure what to ask? Tap a suggestion.

Fun for kids. Calm for parents.

Is it poisonous? Can I touch it? Can I eat it? The answer is always: ask a grown-up.

Parent gate
Purchases, settings and deleting friends sit behind a quick multiplication question.
Daily time limit
Choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. A calm rest screen appears when time is up, and you can grant 15 more.
Every chat, readable
Open the Parent Area to read what was said and correct a friend's name, species or caution level.
Private by design
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Photos, friends and chats stay on the iPhone.
Reminders only if you want them
A daily nudge is available in the Parent Area and off by default.

To recognize a photo and write a reply, the photo and the chat text are processed by AI services. The privacy policy spells out exactly what goes where.

Speaks your child's language.

Natusay follows the language of the iPhone. Characters read, talk and listen in it too. Change it any time in the Parent Area.

A reason to go outside.

  • Daily quests

    “Find a leaf!” or “Spot a bird!” Finishing a quest means meeting a friend of the right kind, so the phone is the excuse and the garden is the point.

  • My Friends

    Everyone you meet is kept with their photo, portrait and chat. Filter by bugs, birds or plants and heart your favorites.

  • Did you know?

    Each friend carries a few true, kid-sized facts, and the home screen surfaces one a day.

Free to start.
