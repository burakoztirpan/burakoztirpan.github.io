About Natusay

Natusay is an iPhone app that lets children photograph living things and objects, and chat with AI-generated characters based on them. It is published by Burak Öztırpan (“we”, “us”). How we handle data is explained in the Privacy Policy.

Parents and supervision

Natusay is designed for children to use together with a parent or guardian. You are responsible for supervising use, for the photos taken, and for purchases. Purchases, settings, deleting friends and time limits are protected by a parent gate, which is a convenience and not a guarantee that a child cannot get past it.

You must be old enough to enter a binding agreement in your country to accept these terms, and to buy a subscription.

AI-generated content

Recognition results, characters, personalities, voices, images and replies are generated by AI. They can be inaccurate, incomplete or unexpected. A character may misidentify a living thing. The app tries to keep characters child-friendly, in character and on topic, but we cannot promise every reply will be correct or suitable.

Parents can correct a friend's name, species and caution level in the Parent Area and read past chats. Please tell us if a character says something wrong or inappropriate.

Safety around nature

Natusay is not a field guide, a medical or poison reference, or a safety tool. Never rely on it to decide whether an animal, plant, mushroom or berry is safe to touch, eat or pick up. When in doubt, keep your distance and ask an adult. Look after children outdoors and respect wildlife and private property.

Subscriptions

Natusay is free to start, with a daily limit on scans and chats. Natusay Premium removes the limits and is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly, with prices shown in the app before you buy.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel it.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.
  • We may change prices or the free limits in the future. Price changes follow Apple's rules, including your consent where required.
