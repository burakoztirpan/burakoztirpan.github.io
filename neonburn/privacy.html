Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the NeonBurn app for iPhone and iPad ("the App"), developed by Burak Öztırpan ("we", "us", "our"), handles information.

The short version: we don't run any server and we don't collect personal data. The camera picture never leaves your device. Your body data and workouts are stored on your device, and in Apple Health only if you allow it. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not ask for an email address, phone number, name or any other personal identifier. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). We have no server that receives data from the App, so we cannot see who uses it or how.

2. Camera

The App uses the camera to count your reps. Each camera frame is analysed on your device with Apple's Vision framework to find the positions of your body joints (shoulders, hips, knees and so on). Only these joint positions are used, in memory, while you work out. Camera images and video are never saved, never uploaded and never shared. You can turn camera access off at any time in iOS Settings → NeonBurn.

3. Data stored on your device

To estimate calories and show your progress, the App stores the following on your device only:

DataWhy
Weight, height, age (stored as birth year) and sex (optional)To estimate the active calories you burn.
Daily calorie goalFor the goal rings and charts.
Workout history: date, length, reps per move, active calories, best comboFor your stats, streaks and records.
Settings such as workout length, tempo, set options, voice coach, music and volumeTo remember your choices.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the App removes it. It may be included in your own encrypted iCloud or computer backups of your device, which are controlled by you and Apple.

4. Apple Health

If you allow it, the App connects to Apple Health (HealthKit):

  • Read: your weight, height, date of birth and biological sex, only to fill in your body data for the calorie estimate.
  • Write: each workout you finish, as Mixed Cardio with its start time, duration and active calories.

Health data is read and written on your device through Apple's HealthKit. We never receive it, and it is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining, or shared with third parties. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app → Sharing → Apps → NeonBurn.

5. Sharing your progress

When you tap Share, the App creates a progress image on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose an app or contact there, and what happens next is governed by the app you share to.

6. Purchases

NeonBurn Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The App only checks with Apple, on your device, whether a subscription is active.

7. Voice coach and sound

Spoken cues are produced by the speech voices built into iOS, on your device. The App does not use the microphone.

8. Children

The App is a general fitness app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS data protection.

10. Your choices

  • Change your body data or goal in the App under Profile.
  • Revoke camera access in iOS Settings, and Health access in the Health app.
  • Delete all App data by deleting the App. Workouts already saved to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
