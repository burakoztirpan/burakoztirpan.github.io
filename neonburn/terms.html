2. Health and safety

NeonBurn is a workout app. Physical exercise carries a risk of falls, collisions, strain and injury. Exercise at your own risk.
  • Check with a doctor before starting any exercise programme, especially if you are pregnant, have a heart, lung, joint or other medical condition, take medication, or have not exercised for a long time.
  • Work out in a clear, well-lit area with enough room in every direction. Remove furniture, pets and anything you could trip over or hit.
  • Place your device on a stable surface where it cannot fall, and do not hold it while exercising.
  • Wear suitable footwear or train on a non-slip floor. Warm up, take breaks and drink water.
