Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tensi mobile application ("the App"), developed by Burak Öztırpan ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. The short version: the App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us. Every correlation Tensi finds is computed on your device, and stays there.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Tensi does not require you to create an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal identifier, and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind. We do not operate a server that receives data from the App, and we have no way to identify you as a user or see what patterns Tensi has found for you.

2. Health Data

With your permission, Tensi reads Health data — including heart rate variability (HRV), resting heart rate, sleep, activity, respiratory rate and related metrics — directly from Apple HealthKit, on your device. This data is used only to compute correlations shown to you inside the App. It is never uploaded, never leaves your device, and Tensi never writes data back to Health.

3. Calendar Data

If you grant Calendar access, Tensi reads meeting counts and durations only, to notice patterns between busy stretches and how you feel. Event titles, notes, locations, and attendee names are never read, never stored, and never leave your device.

4. Location Data

If you grant Location access, Tensi uses your location entirely on-device to notice which recurring places correlate with how you feel. Your location is never shown to you as a street address, never reverse-geocoded to a human-readable place by us, and never transmitted anywhere.

5. Weather Data

Tensi uses Apple's WeatherKit to fetch weather conditions for correlation purposes. This requires sending your approximate location to Apple's weather service to retrieve a forecast — the same way Apple's own Weather app works. Apple's handling of that request is governed by Apple's own privacy practices, not ours; we never see or store the data ourselves.

6. Apple Watch & Widgets

Tensi's iPhone app, Apple Watch app, and home/lock-screen widgets share data locally through Apple's App Group storage mechanism — a private, on-device container used to keep your Watch and widgets in sync with the iPhone app. This data never leaves the App Group and is never transmitted off your device or paired devices.

7. Subscriptions & Purchases

Tensi Pro is offered as an auto-renewable subscription, purchased and managed through Apple's StoreKit and your Apple ID. Payment details are handled entirely by Apple — we never see or store your card information. Apple may share limited, anonymized transaction information with us (such as whether a subscription is active) as part of standard App Store reporting; this is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

8. Notifications

If you enable optional local notifications (for example, a weekly report reminder), they are scheduled and delivered entirely by iOS on your device, using data already stored in the App. No notification data is sent to or processed by us.

9. Third-Party Services

Tensi does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. The only outside service the App communicates with is Apple's own WeatherKit (see Section 5) and Apple's own StoreKit for purchases (see Section 7) — both first-party Apple frameworks, not third-party trackers.

10. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Tensi is rated for ages 9 and up on the App Store.

11. Your Rights

Since we never receive or store your personal data on any server, there is nothing for us to access, export, correct, or delete on your behalf. You are always in full control: revoking a permission in iOS Settings, clearing the App's data, or uninstalling it removes everything immediately, on your device.

12. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the App adds a feature that does process data off-device — we will update this page and the "Effective date" above, and reflect any material change in the App itself before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to: burakoztirpan@gmail.com
