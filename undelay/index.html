FAQ

Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Undelay has no account and no server, and it does not use analytics or ads.

What counts as a kept promise?

A promise counts when you finish it. Dated promises are scored when their date passes. Counted promises earn partial credit, so 7 of 10 push-ups is 70%.

Do I have to set dates?

No. Undated promises count when you finish them. If they wait too long, Undelay shows them as waiting so nothing quietly disappears.

How do I cancel Pro?

In Settings on your iPhone: your name, then Subscriptions. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase.
