FAQ

Questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Undelay has no account and no server, and it does not use analytics or ads.

What counts as a kept promise?

A promise counts when you finish it. Dated promises are scored when their date passes. Counted promises earn partial credit, so 7 of 10 push-ups is 70%.

Do I have to set dates?

No. Undated promises count when you finish them. If they wait too long, Undelay shows them as waiting so nothing quietly disappears.

How do I cancel Pro?

In Settings on your iPhone: your name, then Subscriptions. The lifetime option is a one-time purchase.

What is Undelay?

Undelay is a to-do list and habit app for iPhone and iPad that helps you beat procrastination. Every task is a promise to yourself. Undelay tracks what you finish, postpone and drop, and turns it into one honest number, your Kept Rate, so you can see where you follow through and where you keep delaying. It works without an account and stores everything on your device.

Is there an app that helps you stop procrastinating?

Undelay is built for this. Instead of only listing tasks, it shows how often you actually follow through, which areas of life slip, your hardest weekday and the promises you keep postponing, so procrastination becomes visible and easier to change.

What is the Kept Rate?

The Kept Rate is the share of your promises that you finish. Dated promises are scored when their date passes, and counted promises such as 10 push-ups get partial credit.

Does Undelay support habits and repeating tasks?

Yes. Promises can repeat every day, on certain days, every few days, a few times a week, monthly or yearly, with optional reminders, widgets and Siri or Shortcuts support.

Is Undelay free?

Yes. Unlimited promises, repeats, reminders, labels, widgets and the 7- and 30-day Kept Rate are free. Undelay Pro, available yearly or as a lifetime purchase, adds deeper insights, full history and photo proof.
