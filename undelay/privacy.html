Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Undelay mobile application ("the App"), developed by Burak Öztırpan ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. The short version: we do not operate any server, and we never receive, collect or store your personal data. Everything you enter in Undelay stays on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Undelay does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal identifier. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting software. We have no way to identify you or to see what you add to the App.

2. What the App Stores on Your Device

The promises you create (titles, notes, dates, repeat rules, counts, reminders and labels), their results (kept, partly kept, postponed, dropped or missed), your settings, and any photos you attach. This data is saved in the App's private storage on your iPhone or iPad, inside an App Group container that is shared only with the App's own widgets. It is not uploaded to us or to any third party, and it is not synced to iCloud by the App.

3. Photos

If you choose to attach a photo to a promise, the App asks for access through Apple's photo picker and saves a copy of only the photos you pick in its private storage. Undelay never browses your photo library on its own and never uploads photos anywhere.

4. Notifications

Reminders you set are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. Notification content (such as a promise title) is created on your device and is not sent to us.

5. Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a snapshot of your promises from the App's private storage on the same device. If you use Siri, Shortcuts, the Action Button or Control Center to add or complete a promise, the request is handled by Apple's App Intents on your device. Apple's handling of Siri requests is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

6. Purchases

Undelay Pro is sold through the App Store as an auto-renewable yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, handled by Apple's StoreKit and your Apple Account. Payment details are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store them. Apple may provide us with anonymized, aggregate sales reports as part of standard App Store reporting.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party SDKs and does not share data with third parties. The only outside service it uses is Apple's StoreKit for purchases.

8. Children's Privacy

Because the App collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a promise removes it, its history and its photos. Archiving hides a promise but keeps its history in your score. Uninstalling the App removes all of its data from your device. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing on our side to access, export or delete.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds an optional feature that works with data off the device (such as iCloud sync), we will update this page and the effective date above, and explain the change in the App before it takes effect.
