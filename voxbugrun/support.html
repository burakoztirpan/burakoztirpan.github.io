Getting set up

The game says "Step in front of the camera"

Prop the device up at about hip height and take two big steps back, until the camera sees you from head to knees. Tall players may need to step back a little further. A plain background and good light help.

The hands-up start doesn't react

After the calibration ring fills, raise both hands clearly above your head and hold them there for half a second. Make sure your hands stay inside the picture.

Jumps or ducks are missed

Stay in the middle of the picture, with the lane markers on the camera preview as a guide, and make the moves big: a real jump with both feet, a real squat. Bright, even lighting works best; avoid a bright window behind you.

The camera is turned off

Open iOS Settings → Voxbug Run → Camera and switch it on. You can also play with touch: Parent zone → Settings → Play → Touch (swipe).

We don't have much space

Try Seated mode in the Parent zone settings: lean to change lanes and bend forward to duck. Tiny mode is also slower and doesn't require running on the spot.

Purchases

Restore a purchase on a new device

Parent zone → Store → Restore purchases, while signed in with the same Apple Account used to buy it. Honey packs are consumable and cannot be restored.

Request a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
