Voxbug Run is played by moving: jumping, ducking, stepping and running on the spot. Physical activity carries a risk of falls, collisions and injury.
  • Play in a clear, well-lit area with enough room to move in every direction. Remove furniture, toys, pets and anything else you could trip over or bump into.
  • Place the device on a stable surface where it cannot fall, and do not hold it while playing.
  • Wear suitable footwear or play barefoot on a non-slip floor.
